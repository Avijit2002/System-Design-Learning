##### What are design patterns?

They are proven solutions to recurring design problems in development. They are not code, rather but practices shared into reusable abstractions.


##### Three types of design patterns

- **Creational** - They deals with object creation mechanism.
- **Structural** - focuses on how classes and objects are composed to form larger structure. It helps system to work together that otherwise could not because of incompatible interfaces.
- **Behavioral** - concerned with communication between objects.
